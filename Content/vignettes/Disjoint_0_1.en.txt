Disjoint Molecular Structures and Their Applications

#Chapter I

#The recent discovery of disjoint molecules has been one of the most groundbreaking and peculiar discoveries about the nature of alchemy in recent years. With the use of highly specialized equipment, certain atoms and molecules can be "bonded" into a single molecule without an actual bond combining them. Disjoint molecules, before they were fully understood, were said to contain "ephemeral" or "quantum bonds". However, it is now widely accepted that there is no localized bonding force acting upon various components of a disjoint molecule.


Here, we can see a basic alchemical engine that combines two disjoint molecules with two components into a single disjoint molecule with three components. Any modern alchemical engineer worth their salt should understand how to employ the basic disjoint reagents to design nearly any disjoint atomic structure.
