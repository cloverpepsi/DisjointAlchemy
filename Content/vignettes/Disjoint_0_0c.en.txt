Portraits: Serena, Jerin

Serena: The first class of the term. Are you excited?

Jerin: It seems to be about as exciting as all the other classes I've taken.

Jerin: Which is to say not particularly exciting at all.

Serena: Did you read the textbook ahead of time?

Jerin: I mostly glanced over it. Attempted one of the constructions it described.

Jerin: It was largely uninteresting.

Serena: (playful) You know, I wonder how you even got accepted into the University given your complete _lack_ of enthusiasm for alchemy.

Jerin: Don't be mistaken. I very much want to be an _alchemist_.

Jerin: I'm just not particularly partial to most of the work that needs to be done to get there.

Serena: Mmh. Perhaps. Still, it's impressive that they haven't kicked you out yet.

Jerin: I'm being efficient. Putting in just enough effort to get by.

Serena: (laughing) You're ridiculous. Let's get to class.
