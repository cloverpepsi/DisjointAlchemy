#Although almost all atoms have been shown to be capable of being the basis of a disjoint molecule, salt is the most effective and reliable for forming disjoint molecules over distances greater than the width of an atom. For this reason, the humble salt will be the core of the vast majority of molecules in this book. However, the glyphs of Calcification and Duplication allow turning this salt into one of the cardinals or vice versa, allowing for a greater degree of diversity in the types of disjoint molecules that can be easily manufactured.
